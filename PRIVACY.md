# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

"Scrypted Personal" is a personal, non-commercial app used by a single individual, its developer.

## What the app accesses
With the owner's explicit consent, the app uses the Google Smart Device Management API
(`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/sdm.service`) to read and stream data from the owner's own
Google Nest camera, including live video and snapshots.

## How data is used and stored
- Data is used only to view the owner's own camera on the owner's own home server.
- OAuth tokens are stored on that home server and are never sent to any third party.
- No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.

## Retention and revocation
Data stays on the owner's server. Access can be revoked at any time at
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, and deleting the server's data removes stored tokens.
